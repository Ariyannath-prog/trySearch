Method

We publish our maths.
Ask the others for theirs.

In this category the same word means different arithmetic at every vendor. One says visibility and means presence; another ships a 0–100 composite and never publishes the formula. You cannot reconcile two tools, which is convenient for whoever you bought first.

Visibility Score — the whole formula

published
VS = 100 × ( 0.5 × MentionRate
           + 0.3 × PositionScore
           + 0.2 × CitationRate )
MentionRate
answers naming you ÷ all answers
PositionScore
mean reciprocal rank, mentioned answers only
CitationRate
answers citing your own domain ÷ all answers
Worked example — 100 answers
0.40mentioned in 40× 0.5 = 0.200
0.7171st×20, 2nd×12, 3rd×8× 0.3 = 0.215
0.15own domain cited 15× 0.2 = 0.030
44.5

Weights are constants stored in config and shown in-app. Changing them recomputes history, because raw answers are immutable.

Sentiment is deliberately outside the score — extraction confidence is lowest there, so it is reported separately on a −100…+100 scale.
Share of voice
Your mentions over all tracked-brand mentions, weighted by prompt demand. Unweighted SoV is inflated by tracking prompts nobody asks. Ours cannot be.
Retrieved vs cited
Two metrics, never merged. Retrieved = the model was given your page. Cited = it named it. The gap is usually where the work is.
Confidence interval
Wilson score interval over the period's answers, printed on the figure. At 650 answers that is ±3.6 pts; at 27,000, ±0.6.
Minimum sample
No score below 86 answers for a ±10-point claim, or 345 for ±5. Under that you get collecting data, not a number.
“No measurable change”
Rendered whenever this period's interval overlaps the previous one. We would rather tell you nothing happened than sell you a green arrow.
Variance probe
Ten prompts × five runs, weekly, so single-prompt volatility is measured rather than assumed. Costs us about $6/mo. It is why we can quote an interval at all.
Absent, three ways
not yet run, run and absent, too few runs to say — plus engine not in your slots, which is a fourth and never shown as 0%.
Data provenance
Every engine column carries its source: API, grounded API, or SERP vendor. Google is Gemini-with-grounding until a vendor is added.

Questions people actually ask

What is locked on a cheaper plan — and is it hidden?

Locked, never hidden. On Starter you still see Content Studio, MCP, crawler analytics and the report builder in your sidebar with a padlock; clicking one tells you what it does, what it costs and why it sits where it does — no blurred screenshots of invented data. You can also preview the entire product as any plan from the cards above, which is the fastest way to find out whether the tier up is worth it.

I run an agency. What do I actually get?

On any paid plan you can run a workspace per client and switch between them. On Enterprise you additionally get the client-brand rollup — every brand you manage in one view, ranked by what needs you — and white-label reports with our name removed. That is the first of the agency tooling rather than all of it; bulk actions across brands, per-client seats, cross-brand benchmarks and one invoice are still to come, and we would rather list them as missing than imply they exist. Below Enterprise you still get scheduled reports, they just carry our branding.

Why three fixed engines when others advertise ten?

Because ten engines on a $149 plan is a marketing number, not a product. ChatGPT, Google and Perplexity are reachable by official API, cheap enough to run often, and together they cover the large majority of real AI search. Fixing them on the entry plan is what makes it possible to run them frequently — which matters far more than breadth. Growth unlocks two slots you choose from the other five and swap at will, and any paid plan can buy extra engines outright. What we will not do is sell you eight engines you can only afford to sample once a month.

What is the variance probe?

Ten of your prompts, run five times each, every week. It measures how much a single prompt's answer wobbles, which is what lets us put an honest interval on everything else. It costs us about six dollars a month per account, which is why it is on every plan including Starter. No other tool in this category runs one.

Why is Google labelled “grounded API” and not AI Overviews?

Because it is Gemini with Search grounding, which is a strong proxy for AI Overviews but not literally the same surface. Saying so costs us a little credibility in a demo and buys us a lot the first time a client checks. When we add a licensed SERP vendor for true AI Overviews, the label will change to SERP vendor and your history will be annotated at the switch.

Why can't I get daily runs on Growth?

Frequency is the biggest cost lever in this product by a wide margin. Daily runs on 400 prompts cost us roughly $293 a month against a $449 plan — a 35% margin, which is not enough to keep the lights on once support and infrastructure are paid for. Running every three days on a properly sized prompt set costs about a third as much and still gives you a tighter interval than daily runs on a bloated one. Enterprise gets daily because it is priced per response, so the arithmetic works.

Will you tell me my numbers went nowhere?

Frequently. About a third of period-over-period comparisons in our data are statistically indistinguishable and we label all of them that way. If you want a dashboard that always trends upward, buy a different one — sincerely, it will be cheaper.

What happens in the free scan?

You give a domain. We read your site, infer your category, propose competitors we observed models naming, draft a prompt set, and run it once across your three slots — about a minute, no card, no signup. The competitor and prompt sets are yours to reject; a wrong competitor set makes every downstream number meaningless.
