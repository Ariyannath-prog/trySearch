Agency FAQ

Before you start.

Can I start without a credit card?

Yes. The agency plans are designed with a 14-day trial path before a paid subscription is required.

Can I move from Launch to Growth later?

Yes. You can move to a higher agency capacity as your client portfolio, prompt volume and reporting requirements increase.

Can I manage multiple client projects?

Yes. Launch includes 5 projects, Growth includes 10 and Enterprise Agencies is structured for unlimited projects.

Are MCP, Looker Studio and API access live?

These integrations are part of the product roadmap and are marked Coming soon until their production integrations are deployed and verified.

Can agencies use white-label reports?

White-label reporting is part of the agency product structure, with deeper platform-level white-label functionality planned separately.

Can Enterprise Agencies use custom requirements?

Yes. Enterprise is intended for larger prompt volume, client portfolios, reporting requirements and custom commercial arrangements.
